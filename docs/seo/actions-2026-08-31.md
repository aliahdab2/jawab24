## 1. What was measured (live, 2026-08-31)

### 1.1 The no-store segment is invisible — this is the headline finding

The same question was put to three AI engines twice within one session, changing exactly one
variable: whether the merchant has a store platform.

| Engine | Store framing («لمتاجر سلة وزد») | No-store framing («ما عندي متجر ولا موقع») |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT (web search) | **#1 recommendation**, cited `jawab24.com` ×2 | **absent** — answered ManyChat, then Respond.io |
| Google AI Mode | in comparison table, cited, **tier 3 of 3** | **absent** — answered ManyChat, Chatfuel, ZaBot, Widgix |
| Microsoft Copilot | **absent** — answered Respond.io, ManyChat, Wati | **cited but not recommended** (see §1.3) |
| Google organic (gl=SA, hl=ar) | #1 and #5 | #8, then outside top 10 |

Google AI Mode described ZaBot and Widgix as «توفر حلولاً مخصصة للتجار الذين يبيعون عبر
السوشيال ميديا فقط». Copilot called Raabt AI «الخيار رقم 1 بلا منافس» and justified it partly
with «لا يحتاج متجر إلكتروني». That is our proposition, awarded to competitors, because we
never wrote it down.

**Root cause — a corpus problem, not a ranking problem.** `site:jawab24.com` restricted to the
no-store vocabulary (بدون متجر / بدون موقع / مطعم / عيادة / خدمات) returns **2 URLs out of 106**:

- `/terms` — which still defines the service as Facebook-page and Instagram auto-reply, with no
  WhatsApp and no store-free framing. It is indexed, and engines read it as a definition.
- `/blog/case-study-damascus-training-institute` — one passing sentence.

Every store platform has a landing page (`/integrations/salla`, `/zid`, `/shopify`). The segment
with no platform has none. `frontend/src/data/integrations.ts` still lists only the three stores.

**The irony worth acting on:** our single best proof asset is already a no-store customer.
Al-Fariq Al-Dimashqi is a training institute — no Salla, no Zid, no Shopify — with 44,636
replies and 472 attributed leads. It is currently filed as a generic case study.

### 1.2 The WhatsApp corpus gap, counted per field

All 36 blog posts were fetched and WhatsApp mentions counted **separately** in `seoTitle`,
`seoDescription`, the H1 (`title`) and the article body — because the first three are what
retrieval weights and the body is what gets summarised.

| Tier | Arabic | English | WhatsApp in seoTitle / seoDescription / title | Body mentions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp-native | 3 of 18 | 3 of 18 | yes | 10–18 |
| **WhatsApp-blind** | **15 of 18** | **15 of 18** | **none** | 1–4, in 640–1,530 words |

In the Arabic tree exactly one blind post (`ai-auto-reply-customer-photos`) mentions WhatsApp in
its `seoDescription`; **zero** mention it in `seoTitle` or `title`. The 1–4 body mentions are a
boilerplate CTA line, not content — a 1,527-word post can name WhatsApp once, in the footer.

WhatsApp-native today: `whatsapp-auto-reply-jawab24`, `jawab24-data-security`,
`jawab24-setup-tutorial`.

**This is the mechanism behind AI Mode's tier-3 placement.** An engine summarising this corpus
sees 30 documents about Facebook/Instagram comment automation and 3 about WhatsApp, and concludes
— correctly, given the evidence — that WhatsApp is a side feature. Its comparison row listed our
primary channel as "Instagram and Facebook" while rivals got "WhatsApp + multi-channel".

Confirms and quantifies §1.3 of the 2026-08-22 doc ("There is no public WhatsApp page").

### 1.3 Copilot cites our content to sell a competitor

Run from the founder's signed-in account, so this is a real result rather than a logged-out
approximation.

- **Store question:** Jawab24 not mentioned. Every citation in the entire answer came from
  **respond.io's own blog** — no Arabic sources, no Salla/Zid pages, no local vendors. Copilot's
  retrieval on that query is one English SaaS company's content marketing.
- **No-store question:** recommended Raabt AI #1, ManyChat #2 — and the source cited for the
  ManyChat write-up was **`jawab24.com/blog/best-auto-reply-tools-2026`**.

Our comparison post was mined to describe a competitor while we were left out of the
recommendation. That is the predictable cost of writing comparison content in a neutral
third-party voice: the engine reads it as reference material *about other tools* and never
registers the publisher as a candidate. The post ranks — as a source, not as a product.

### 1.4 Positioning: the framing exists, the proof does not

Worth recording, because it changes the fix. The "salesman, not a bot" positioning is **already
on the site and it is good**:

- H1: «جواب24 — حوّل صفحتك إلى آلة بيع ذكية 24/7» — *your page*, no store implied.
- meta description opens: «أذكى من بوتات الرد التلقائي: موظف ذكي…»
- `/what-is-jawab24`: «جواب24 ليس شات بوت».

The defect is not a missing frame. It is that the frame is immediately followed by
«يتكامل مع شوبيفاي وسلة وزد» and is unsupported by any no-store evidence anywhere on the site.

**The trap to avoid while fixing it.** "We're not a bot" is a category-breaking claim, and
category-breaking claims cost retrieval: we cannot be chosen for a query we do not appear in.
The data says the bot vocabulary is not the problem — we rank **#1** for «ربط متجر سلة ببوت رد
تلقائي» (contains بوت) and are **absent from the top 20** for «رد تلقائي على تعليقات فيسبوك».
Not competing in that vocabulary is the problem.

Split the job by surface:

- **SEO — their words.** Google matches strings. `seoTitle` / `title` / `seoKeywords` carry
  بوت، شات بوت، رد تلقائي، بدون متجر. This is how we get in the room.
- **GEO — our words.** AI engines match *situation*, not keyword. The body opens with the
  reframe. This is where "salesman, not bot" wins.

Order matters: match the query, then break the category, on the same page.

**Missing vocabulary to add verbatim.** «كم السعر؟» «في مقاسات؟» «متوفر؟» and "50–100 messages a
day" appear nowhere on the site outside one line in the Damascus post. That phrasing is precisely
how a merchant describes the problem to ChatGPT, and it is the strongest GEO asset we have.

## 2. Plan — 4 to 6 weeks

Ordered by ratio of effect to effort, not by size. Tracks A and D run in parallel from day one
because D has external lead time.

### Track A — the no-store track *(week 1–2, blocking nothing, blocked by nothing)*

The largest gap and the one entirely within our control. Currently at literal zero on every AI
engine.

- **A1. `/no-store` landing page, ar + en.** The channel-page pattern already exists
  (`/instagram`, added #790) — follow it. `seoTitle` in the seller's vocabulary
  («بوت رد تلقائي لفيسبوك وإنستغرام بدون متجر إلكتروني»); body opens with the reframe. Include
  «كم السعر؟» «في مقاسات؟» «متوفر؟» verbatim and the 50–100/day volume claim.
- **A2. Add a no-store entry to `frontend/src/data/integrations.ts`** — or, if that file is
  strictly store-platform-shaped, a sibling "channels" list so the page is reachable from the
  same navigation as Salla/Zid/Shopify. A merchant with no store must see a path that is not
  an integration.
- **A3. Reframe `case-study-damascus-training-institute`** as the flagship no-store proof.
  Lead the `title`/`excerpt` with "a training institute with no online store". Same numbers,
  different framing. This is the highest-credibility asset we have for the segment.
- **A4. Segment posts** for the business types that live entirely on a Facebook page —
  restaurants, clinics, training centres, salons, tutors. Two to start, not five.
- **A5. Add a "no store required" Q&A to the landing-page FAQPage schema.**
- **A6. Update `llms.txt`** so the audience line names social-only sellers *first*, ahead of
  e-commerce. Currently leads with e-commerce owners.
- **A7. Fix `/terms`** — it still defines the product as Facebook/Instagram auto-reply. Indexed,
  and read as a definition.

*Acceptance:* asking ChatGPT and Google AI Mode the no-store question in Arabic returns
`jawab24.com` as a cited source. Recommendation position is a stretch goal, citation is the gate.

### Track B — WhatsApp retrofit *(week 1–3, can interleave with A)*

30 of 36 posts carry no WhatsApp signal in the fields that matter. Do not attempt all 30.

**The seven highest-weight posts, in order:**

| Post (ar + en) | Why it is first |
|---|---|

| `case-study-damascus-training-institute` | also Track A3 — do both edits in one pass |

- **B1.** For each: rewrite `seoTitle`, `seoDescription`, `title` and the opening paragraph to
  name all three channels. Add `seoKeywords` entries for WhatsApp.
- **B2.** Add a WhatsApp-specific step or worked example *inside* the article body — not a CTA
  line at the end. The body is what gets summarised.
- **B3. Leave the slugs alone.** Three of these have `facebook` baked into the URL. Renaming
  costs redirects and ranking risk for a signal that `seoTitle`/`title`/body carries perfectly
  well. Recorded as a deliberate decision, not an oversight.
- **B4. A public WhatsApp channel page** on the `/instagram` pattern. The launch post's H1
  («واتساب متاح الآن في جواب24») is a news headline, not a query anyone types — flagged in
  08-22 §1.3 and still true.
- **B5.** Confirm `<lastmod>` regenerates for every touched post (the generated sitemap from
  08-22 §A should handle this) and fire `scripts/indexnow-ping.sh`. Engines will not re-summarise
  until they re-fetch.

*Acceptance:* Google AI Mode's channel description for Jawab24 names WhatsApp. Re-run the §3
query set at week 4 and week 6 — this one is expected to lag the content by 2–4 weeks.

### Track C — comparison pages: be the answer, not the reference *(week 2–3)*

Smallest change, highest ratio, direct evidence from §1.3.

- **C1.** On `/compare`, the five `/compare/*` pages and `best-auto-reply-tools-2026`: make the
  Jawab24 row the most extractable object on the page — named verdict near the top, a
  one-sentence "best for" claim in our own voice, a self-contained summary block an engine can
  lift whole.
- **C2.** Add `SoftwareApplication` (or `Product`) markup **for Jawab24** on those pages, so the
  publisher is machine-readable as a candidate and not only as an author.
- **C3.** Keep the honest assessments of rivals. They are why the pages get cited at all. The
  fix is where the citation lands, not the tone.

*Acceptance:* Copilot's no-store answer, re-run, cites `jawab24.com` for a Jawab24 claim rather
than for a ManyChat claim.

### Track E — third-party citations *(week 3–6, ongoing)*

The currency Perplexity trades in and the one we have none of. Cannot be manufactured quickly,

### Track F — entity consolidation *(week 4, small)*

- **F1.** Pick one Instagram handle; redirect attention to it.
- **F2.** Add Google Play, LinkedIn and the second Instagram to `sameAs`.

### Deferred — the head-term cluster

#### التوصية

**افهرِس `/whatsapp` العربية.** نسختها الإنجليزية زُحف إليها خلال ٢٢ ساعة، بينما العربية غائبة عن

الذي يتيح لمساعدٍ ذكي أن يجيب عن سؤال «بدون متجر + واتساب» بنا نحن بدل أدوات Meta المدمجة. أعد
إرسال IndexNow لهذا الرابط، واطلب فهرسته في Search Console، وتأكّد أنه ليس يتيماً في الروابط
الداخلية كما كادت أن تكون `/no-store`.
