## The Gap Between Where Customers Ask and Where Products Live

If you sell on Salla, your marketing likely lives on Facebook and Instagram. You run ads, post product photos, and share promotions. Customers discover your products on social media — but your product catalog, prices, and inventory live in Salla.

This creates a gap: when a customer comments "How much is the blue one?" on your Facebook post, you need to check Salla, find the product, get the current price, and reply. If you have 50 comments a day, that's hours of manual work.

Worse, if you don't reply fast enough, the customer moves on. Studies show that businesses that respond within **5 minutes** are **21x more likely** to convert a lead than those who respond after 30 minutes.

The solution: connect your Salla product catalog directly to your Facebook auto-reply system. When a customer asks about a product, the AI checks your real Salla data and replies instantly with accurate information.

## What Product Sync Actually Does

When you connect your Salla store to an auto-reply tool like Jawab24, here's what happens:

**Product import:** Your entire Salla catalog is imported — product names, descriptions, prices, images, and availability. This becomes part of the AI's Knowledge Base.

**Automatic updates:** When you change a price or mark a product as out of stock in Salla, the auto-reply system updates automatically through webhooks. No manual sync needed.

**Bilingual data:** If your Salla products have Arabic and English descriptions, both versions are synced. The AI uses whichever language matches the customer's message.

**Price verification:** This is critical. When the AI generates a reply mentioning a price, it checks the actual Salla catalog before sending. If the price doesn't match, the reply is held for review. This prevents the AI from inventing or guessing prices.

The result: when a customer asks "Do you have this in black?" or "What's the price of the leather bag?", the AI checks your real Salla inventory and replies with accurate, current information.

## Step-by-Step: Connecting Salla to Jawab24

The connection process takes under 2 minutes:

1. **Go to Settings, then Integrations** in your Jawab24 dashboard
2. **Click "Connect Salla"** — this opens Salla's OAuth authorization page
3. **Authorize the connection** — click Approve on Salla's page. No API keys to copy, no code to write
4. **Wait for sync** — your products are imported automatically. For a typical store with 100-500 products, this takes about 30 seconds
5. **Verify your products** — check the Integrations page to see your synced products, prices, and availability
6. **Test it** — comment on one of your Facebook posts asking about a specific product. The AI should reply with accurate data from your Salla store

After the initial sync, updates happen automatically. When you add a new product or change a price in Salla, it's reflected in auto-replies within minutes.
