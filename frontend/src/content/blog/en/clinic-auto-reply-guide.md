## The same two questions, fifty times a day

Every clinic and beauty center active on social media knows this day: one post about a new offer, and the inbox fills with dozens of near-identical messages — "How much is a session?", "When are you open?", "Where exactly are you located?", "Is there an appointment tomorrow?". The questions arrive at every hour, the receptionist is busy with the patients actually inside the clinic, and the customer who doesn't get a quick answer asks the next clinic in the search results.

The problem isn't just the volume — it's the nature of it: ninety percent of these messages carry the same questions, and the answers never change. That is exactly the kind of work you can delegate.

## Why manual replies aren't enough — and neither is a traditional "bot"

Traditional auto-reply bots match keywords: if the customer types "price", they send a canned price list. But clinic customers don't write that way — they ask "my daughter is 12, is braces treatment suitable for her?" or send a voice note while driving. A keyword catches none of that.

The difference with an AI employee like [Jawab24](/en/what-is-jawab24) is that it doesn't match keywords — it understands the question: it reads the customer's message (text, or a voice note transcribed to text) and answers from the Business Info you wrote once: your services and prices, working hours, address, and booking policy. And what it doesn't know, it doesn't invent: low-confidence replies are held for your review instead of being sent.

## What does the AI employee answer in a clinic?

Examples of what actually recurs:

- **"How much is a laser session?"** — answers with the price written in your info, in the customer's language and dialect.
- **"What are your hours on Friday?"** — answers from your registered working hours; outside them, it sends the away message you configured.
- **"Where are you located?"** — sends the address and directions you wrote.
- **A voice note** — is transcribed, the question is understood, and the answer arrives as a text message.

All of it on all three channels: Facebook comments and Messenger, Instagram comments and DMs, and WhatsApp Business messages — from one dashboard and one set of information.
